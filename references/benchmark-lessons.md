# Benchmark Lessons

Use this reference only when benchmark evidence is available or a narrative-form judgment needs support. Borrow dependency principles, not domain wording, fixed section labels, citation quotas, or project-specific claims.

## Complete positive exemplars

### zy09

The strongest transferable lesson is cross-section isomorphism. Multiple research questions keep a stable order from Introduction through theoretical development, methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion. The Results progression moves from descriptive grounding and diagnostics to RQ answers and robustness. Transfer the stable claim-return order and theory-to-question progression; do not copy its domain constructs.

### zy03

The strongest lesson is the narrowing funnel: practical failure creates a need for a technical capability; that capability exposes an institutional or mechanism boundary; closest work then isolates the exact unresolved intersection. Transfer the rule that a technology or method enters only after the prior problem makes it necessary. Use controlled comparisons and precise closest-work positioning rather than generic novelty language.

### Wang et al.

The strongest lesson is the explanation ladder. The paper moves from an implementation problem through actor motivation and dynamic mechanism to a governance choice, then uses Discussion to convert apparently conflicting findings into a bounded strategic trade-off. Transfer the symmetry between problem decomposition, literature streams, method requirements, and interpretation. Treat publication status as evidence of a complete worked narrative, not a universal template.

## Planning evidence only

### zy08

The available front-end material usefully reframes an effectiveness question into a mechanism or design question. Because the later sections are incomplete, it can inform opening moves and planned dependencies only. It cannot demonstrate successful Results-to-Conclusion return, mature citation practice, or final length.

### zy07

The planned chain from predictive performance to decision quality is useful as a structural hypothesis. Skeletal sections do not establish that the handoffs work in prose or that evidence closes the claims. Use it to generate questions for an audit, never as positive proof that a narrative pattern succeeds.

## Negative exemplar

### zy05

The transferable warning is version drift. Locally coherent technical dependencies cannot rescue a paper whose global idea, terminology, or later chapters belong to different research versions. A strong technical chain may therefore coexist with a failed whole-paper story. Always run the version-consistency gate before rewarding section-level coherence.

## Distilled rules

Across the complete exemplars, the durable pattern is:

`consequential phenomenon -> practical mechanism/tension -> problem-oriented literature streams -> exact gap and consequence -> RQs -> capability-matched method -> validity before claims -> results in promise order -> explanation and literature dialogue -> bounded action -> closure`

Apply four cautions:

1. Evidence bins and search directions are not automatically manuscript subsections.
2. Finished exemplars support functional ordering, not fixed paragraph counts or wording.
3. Citation density varies by sentence function; it is not a quality target.
4. Incomplete plans are labeled as planning evidence, and negative examples teach a failure mode rather than a preferred form.
